01 — The Vision

A village that steps
down to the sea

“On the Amalfi Coast there is a town where every home leans towards the water, terrace upon terrace, until the streets end in the sea. They call it Positano.”

Positano Residences brings that romance to the Arabian Gulf: homes that descend to a private beach in planted terraces — every roof the next garden, no home behind another, the sea always in front of you.

02 — Architecture

Terraces that belong to the landscape

I. The Terraces

Villas step down with the dune — each roof is the garden of the home above.

II. The Boulevard

A promenade of boutiques and cafés in terracotta and travertine, leading straight to the water.

III. The Beach Pavilion

The residents' club on the sand — pool, spa, gym and lounge behind a single pane of glass.

Master-planned and designed in-house by Qube Development.
