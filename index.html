01 — The Vision

A village that steps
down to the sea

“On the Amalfi Coast there is a town where every home leans towards the water, terrace upon terrace, until the streets end in the sea. They call it Positano.”

Positano Residences brings that romance to the Arabian Gulf: homes that descend to a private beach in planted terraces — every roof the next garden, no home behind another, the sea always in front of you.
