01 — The Vision

A village that steps
down to the sea

“On the Amalfi Coast there is a town where every home leans towards the water, terrace upon terrace, until the streets end in the sea. They call it Positano.”

Positano Residences brings that romance to the Arabian Gulf: a terraced beachfront community on Al Marjan Island where homes descend the landscape in planted steps — every roof the next garden, every window an open horizon.

No towers casting shadows. No home behind another. A boulevard of boutiques and cafés below, a glass beach pavilion on the sand, and the sea — always — in front of you.

02 — Architecture

Terraces that belong to the landscape

I. The Terraces

Villas step down with the dune, so each roof becomes the garden of the home above. Deep shaded terraces, plunge pools and native planting blur the line between architecture and shore.

II. The Boulevard

At street level, a quiet promenade of boutiques, cafés and arrival lobbies — framed in terracotta and travertine — leads the eye straight to the water. Cooled parking disappears below.

III. The Beach Pavilion

On the sand itself, a glass-lined pavilion holds the residents' beach club: pool, spa, gym and lounge, with nothing between you and the Gulf but a pane of glass.

Master-planned and designed in-house by Qube Development.
